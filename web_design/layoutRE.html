Quick Security Tips

Verify the Domain: Always double-check the website address for typos or unusual characters.

Look for HTTPS: A lock icon in the address bar means the connection is encrypted, but does not guarantee the site is safe.

Beware of Urgency: Phishing attacks often create a sense of panic. Be suspicious of urgent requests for personal info.
